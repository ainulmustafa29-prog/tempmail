About TempMailBox

Free, instant disposable email addresses for privacy-conscious users.

What We Do

TempMailBox is a free, user-friendly service that instantly generates disposable email addresses. Our goal is to protect your privacy by offering secure, anonymous inboxes to avoid spam and keep your real inbox clean. Whether you're testing an app, signing up for a newsletter, or accessing a one-time resource, TempMailBox provides a fast and efficient solution.

Why We Exist

Every day, people share their personal email addresses with websites, apps, and services — often without knowing how that data will be used. Marketing lists, data breaches, and spam are common consequences. We built TempMailBox to give everyone a simple way to protect their primary inbox without sacrificing convenience.

How Temporary Email Works

When you visit TempMailBox, a unique temporary email address is automatically assigned to you. No registration is required. Incoming emails are displayed in your browser inbox in real time. Each mailbox has a limited lifespan and is automatically deleted after expiration to ensure privacy and minimize data retention.

Privacy Philosophy

We believe privacy should be accessible to everyone. TempMailBox is designed with a privacy-first approach:

  • No account registration or personal information required
  • Temporary storage with automatic deletion
  • No tracking of your email content for advertising
  • Transparent policies about data handling

Appropriate Use

TempMailBox is intended for legitimate and ethical use — protecting your privacy during sign-ups, testing, and one-time verifications. We do not tolerate spam, phishing, fraud, malware distribution, or any illegal activity. Misuse of the service may result in access restrictions.

TempMailBox is ideal for receiving one-time verification codes, newsletter confirmations, or trial sign-ups. It is not suitable for banking, healthcare, or other sensitive accounts where long-term access and security guarantees are essential.
